👨‍👩‍👧 Tips for parents

  • Little and often: 10–15 minutes a day works better than one long session.
  • Follow the lessons in order. Each one builds on the last.
  • If you can, use a real soroban as well. Kids learn the finger movements best with real beads.
  • Say the rule out loud together: "plus 4: plus 5, minus 1". Saying it helps it stick.
  • Once a level feels easy, try it without looking at the beads: picture the abacus in your mind. That's "mental abacus" (anzan).

📚 Lessons

1Meet the abacus

  • Frame: the box that holds everything.
  • Beam: the bar across the middle.
  • Rods: the sticks the beads slide on. Each rod is one place: ones, tens, hundreds…
  • Heaven bead (above the beam): worth 5.
  • Earth beads (below the beam): worth 1 each. There are 4 of them.
  • Unit dots: the white dots on the beam help you find your place.
⭐ The golden rule: a bead only counts when it is touching the beam.

2Counting 0 to 9 on one rod

Push earth beads up to make 1, 2, 3, 4. For 5, push the heaven bead down and send all the earth beads away. Then 6 = 5 + 1, 7 = 5 + 2, and so on.

Your turn: make the number on this rod

3Big numbers: ones, tens, hundreds

Each rod has a place value. The rod on the far right of your number is the ones, the next one to the left is the tens, then hundreds, then thousands. Read the rods from left to right, just like reading a number.

💡 Use a unit dot as your ones rod. That way you always know where your number ends.

4Using your fingers

MoveFinger
Push earth beads up (adding 1–4)👍 Thumb
Push earth beads down (taking away 1–4)☝️ Pointer finger
Push the heaven bead down (+5) or up (−5)☝️ Pointer finger
Adding 6, 7, 8 or 9 in one go🤏 Thumb and pointer pinch together

Always work from left to right: start with the biggest place (like the tens) and finish with the ones. Clear the abacus by sweeping all the beads away from the beam before every new sum.

5Easy adding and taking away

When there are enough beads, just move them! This is called a direct sum. Press an example, then press Next step to watch.

6Small friends (friends of 5)

Sometimes there aren't enough earth beads. Try 4 + 3: all 4 earth beads are already up, so there are none left to push! So we use the heaven bead and a small friend. Small friends are two numbers that add up to 5:

1 + 4 = 52 + 3 = 5

To add…do this
+1+5 −4
+2+5 −3
+3+5 −2
+4+5 −1
To take away…do this
−1+4 −5
−2+3 −5
−3+2 −5
−4+1 −5

7Big friends (friends of 10)

When a rod gets too full, we need the rod next door! Big friends are two numbers that add up to 10:

1 + 92 + 83 + 74 + 65 + 5

To add…do this
+9−1 +10
+8−2 +10
+7−3 +10
+6−4 +10
+5−5 +10
+4…+1−6…−9 +10
To take away…do this
−9−10 +1
−8−10 +2
−7−10 +3
−6−10 +4
−5−10 +5
−4…−1−10 +6…+9

"+10" means pushing up one earth bead on the rod to the left (the tens rod).

8Mixed friends (both friends together)

Sometimes a big friend needs help from a small friend! Try 6 + 6: the big friend rule says −4 +10. But to take away 4 from 6, we need the small friend: +1 −5. So 6 + 6 = +1 −5 +10 = 12.

9Two-digit and three-digit sums

Exactly the same rules! Work left to right: do the tens rod first, then the ones rod. Each rod uses its own direct, small friend or big friend move.
